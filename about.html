Marrying craftsmanship with conscientious manufacturing has been our compass since 1948.

Our Scale

150+ professionals. One integrated campus. Zero external bottlenecks.

From yarn texturising and weaving to digital printing and automated embroideries, explore our vertically integrated manufacturing facility in Surat, India.

Our Focus

Three things we will never compromise on.

Sustainability

Our entire manufacturing unit was conceived and built around the goal of green production. Responsible sourcing, reduced waste, and lower carbon outputs aren’t add-ons — they are foundational to how we operate.

Quality

A strict focus on fabric quality — tested at every stage — means on-time, every-time delivery to global buyers. Our standards meet the expectations of the world’s most demanding fashion retail groups.

Agility

With every textile process handled in-house, we move from concept to finished fabric faster than most. That end-to-end control translates directly into better product development speed for our buyers.

Our Team & Approach

Family-run leadership.
Personally invested in every metre.

As a family-run business, we bring a level of personal attention to each customer’s textile requirements that larger, impersonal manufacturers simply cannot match. Unlike corporate conglomerate mills where your brief shifts through layers of detached management, our promoters and senior technical leads stay directly involved from initial yarn selection and sample development to bulk processing and global export dispatch.
