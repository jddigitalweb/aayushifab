About Us

Fashion-forward fabrics.
Built for a better world.

A sustainable polyester fabric manufacturer supplying the global fashion industry — thoughtfully, responsibly, since 1948.

We build products that let fashion brands move faster, greener, and with greater confidence.

From yarns to finished fabric — every process runs in-house at our Surat facility, keeping quality tight and lead times short for buyers worldwide.

Our Roots

From Kolkata, 1948.
Built on trust.

What began as Gourishankar Tulsiram Trading Company in Kolkata has grown, under the stewardship of CEO Mr. Ashok Agarwal, into one of India's trusted sustainable polyester fabric manufacturers.

His vision — to marry craftsmanship with conscientious manufacturing — remains the compass for every decision we make.

Mr. Ashok Agarwal — CEO, Aayushi Fab

Our Scale

150+ people.
Every process, in-house.

Our team of 150+ professionals operates across the full textile value chain — from texturising yarns and knitting and weaving raw fabric, to digital printing, foil printing, and embroideries — all under one roof in Surat, India.

Our Focus

Three things we will never compromise on.

Sustainability

Our entire manufacturing unit was conceived and built around the goal of green production. Responsible sourcing, reduced waste, and lower carbon outputs aren’t add-ons — they are foundational to how we operate.

Quality

A strict focus on fabric quality — tested at every stage — means on-time, every-time delivery to global buyers. Our standards meet the expectations of the world’s most demanding fashion retail groups.

Agility

With every textile process handled in-house, we move from concept to finished fabric faster than most. That end-to-end control translates directly into better product development speed for our buyers.
