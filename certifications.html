Every claim we make about sustainability, chemical safety, and ethical production is backed by rigorous, independent third-party certifications and verified audit frameworks.

Global Recycled Standard (GRS)

All Aayushi Fab facilities are GRS certified, underpinning our sustainable recycled polyester fabric production and preferred-buyer status for certified recycled yarns.

OEKO-TEX® Standard 100

Certificate no. 20.HIN.22445 HOHENSTEIN HTTI. Rigorously tested to ensure all manufactured fabrics are free from harmful chemical substances and ecologically safe.

Higg Facility Social & Labor Module

Verified module under the Sustainable Apparel Coalition, demonstrating measurable compliance with fair labor practices, workplace safety, and social responsibility (Completed 2021-10-22).

R|Elan™ HEP Partner

Selected in 2017 as an authorized Hub Excellence Program (HEP) Partner for Reliance Industries' R|Elan high-performance yarn range (“Fabric 2.0: The Future of Fabrics”).

Independently audited and certified by Control Union for supply chain integrity, material traceability, and continuous environmental management compliance.
